# Data-Analysis-Portfolio-Projects
This is a collection of portfolio projects I have made while studying the field of data analysis. I am practicing a combination of MySQL and Microsoft Power BI, along with writing reports that could be treated as deliverables or used as presentation aides.

## Structure
Currently, there are two main folders in this repo, as I have only put together two projects. They are labeled numerically and are intended to be examined in the order I made them, so you can see my learning process and how I have grown.

Each folder contains a Power BI report, that I sadly cannot publish or share officially as I do not have a business email associated with Power BI. I encourage readers to download and view these reports, click around, and examine my data and DAX queries. With each Power BI report, there is also a written Word Document meant to accompany them. They are intended to be read side by side and hold all the context of why I am mapping what data I am. Additionally, each folder contains the dataset I used, and some folders may have extra content that went along with the dataset, such as MySQL explorations.

## Project Format
Since I am trying to learn data analysis in more than just a casual sense, I have tried to frame these projects as if they are a business deliverable. I am challenging myself by coming up with a set of questions to answer from my data sources, then having ChatGPT role play as a stake holder to come up with five additional questions so I can get a different perspective. I do want to be clear that this is a demonstration of my own abilities, and my capacity to research and learn. AI was only used for question generation, any challenges or roadblocks I came up against were solved by reading program documentation, watching tutorials, experimenting with Stack Overflow and Microsoft forums, or delving into the bowels of statistics websites.
